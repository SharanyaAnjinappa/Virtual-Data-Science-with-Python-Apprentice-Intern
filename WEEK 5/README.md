🔍 Major Findings

Some important findings from the analysis include:

Overall survival rate was approximately 38.38%.
Female passengers had considerably higher survival rates than male passengers.
First-class passengers had higher survival rates than second- and third-class passengers.
Gender and survival showed a statistically significant association.
The Chi-Square test produced a p-value of approximately 1.20 × 10⁻⁵⁸.
The final Logistic Regression model achieved:
Accuracy: 80.45%
Precision: 79.31%
Recall: 66.67%
F1 Score: 72.44%
ROC-AUC: 84.36%
🤖 Machine Learning

A Logistic Regression classification model was developed to predict whether a passenger survived.

The model was evaluated using:

Confusion Matrix
Accuracy
Precision
Recall
F1 Score
ROC-AUC

The ROC-AUC score of 0.8436 indicates that the model was able to distinguish between survivors and non-survivors reasonably well.
